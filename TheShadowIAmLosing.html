THE SHADOW I AM LOSING

A confession written through fading light

I am writing this right now because I have finally run out of places to hide, and honestly, my hands won’t stop shaking. I just need you to look at me and see the truth. I feel like someone who doesn’t know how to do anything but watch things fall apart.

I spend so much energy fighting for things that were never mine to keep, and I end up standing in the middle of the mess, completely losing track of who I even am. I’ve spent so much time pretending to be okay, acting like someone I’m not, just because it’s easier than admitting how lost I feel inside.

But the hardest thing to look at right now is us. The one person I care about the most, the one I am terrified of losing... is you.

THE LOST THING

Remember that story I told you? About going to a place we love, searching everywhere for that one specific thing you want more than anything, only to realize it’s just completely gone? You can’t find it anywhere.

That is exactly how I feel. I am that lost item, just drifting around. And yet, when I was completely swallowed up by that darkness, you were the only one who actually figured me out.

You saw right through every single mask I put up. You didn’t just watch me struggle; you reached in, pulled me out of it, and pushed me away from that dark place. You became my shadow. You stayed with me.

THE INEVITABLE

But that is exactly what makes this hurt so bad right now. How am I supposed to just go back to normal knowing that the person who pulled me out of the dark is the very person who is slipping away from my life? How do I go back to the shadows after you showed me what it felt like to be cared for?

It feels like everything inside me is slowly breaking, and I am just standing here, completely powerless to stop it. And that’s the real ghost I am living with right now. It’s not just the fear of a sudden goodbye. It’s worse than that. It’s the heavy, quiet weight of knowing the truth and trying to force myself to accept it: I am going to lose you.

It might not happen completely today. It might not happen tomorrow. But I can feel it coming. Somewhere down the road, there is a day waiting where you won’t be my shadow anymore.

It feels so incredibly cruel that someone who was already so lost was allowed to find you, only to have to live with the daily fear of watching you fade out of my life. Every morning I wake up with this heavy, choking feeling, knowing I’m just counting down the days.

I feel like I’m standing on something that’s collapsing right beneath my feet, looking at the person who saved me, and having to accept the brutal truth that one day soon, I will be standing here entirely alone again.

✦ ✦ ✦

I am crying as I write this, completely broken by the thought of it, because out of everything I have ever lost... losing you is the one thing I don’t know how to survive.

THE END OF A SHADOW
