Happy Birthday Nanna ❤️
Sometimes I really sit and think… how did a person whom I met only once become one of the biggest parts of my life for almost 5 years?
jeevitham anna tarwatha chala parichayalu jaruguthai.. ala chala mandi mana life loki vachi velthuntaaru kani okallo iddaro matrame mana life lo ala undi pootharu naa life lo ala parichayam ayyi undipoina ekaika person nuvve.
Manam chala takkuva kalisam physically… but honestly, it never felt that way. We met in late night calls, random chats, silence, pain, dreams, chaos, happiness, tears, overthinking, fights, and in every small moment where life felt too heavy. Konni relations untayi… distance tho weak avvavu. Instead they grow roots deeper than presence itself. Ours is one of them.
You know something? Out of all the people I met in my life, nee place evaru touch cheyyaleru. Because no one can love like you do. Your love is confusing sometimes, your care is impossible to explain, your patience changes like seasons 😂, but your loyalty… god, once you decide someone is yours, you hold them with your whole heart. Adi chala rare.
And I know… I hurt you.
Maybe more than I should have. Maybe more than someone who loves you ever should. I still hate remembering those days where you cried because of me. Nijam cheppali ante, even when I acted different outside, inside I felt terrible watching us become distant emotionally. When I needed you most and felt neglected, I changed… then when you needed me, I became distant too. We both silently hurt each other while loving each other the same way underneath everything.
But one thing I can never forget is this — even when I didn’t care properly, even when I failed to show up correctly, you still stayed.
And honestly Nanna… that breaks me sometimes.
Because people usually leave when things become difficult. But you stayed through my confusion, my behavior changes, my silence, my immaturity, my pain… through everything. Thank you feels too small for that.
You know what’s funny? Sometimes it feels like we already lived together emotionally. Like two people sitting side by side holding hands while the whole world keeps moving around them. Even after years, somehow you still became my comfort place.
But thank you for staying. Even when I became distant. Even when my behavior changed. Even when I failed to express what was happening inside me. You still stayed beside me emotionally… like someone silently holding my hand in darkness.
Do you know what the most dangerous thing about you is?
You became home.
And once a person becomes home, every distance starts feeling temporary.
Nanna… if life is kind to me, I want this bond forever. I want years with you. More memories. More midnight talks. More fights. More patch ups. More “yedava” moments 😂❤️
But also thank you too… for not giving up on this yedava 😂❤️
And today, on your birthday, more than anything else… I just want you to feel loved. Truly loved. Not for what you do for people. Not for how much pain you hide. Not for how strong you act. But simply because you are YOU.
I don’t know what destiny has written for us. But I know this much — I want to be there in your life for a very, very long time. Not temporarily. Not halfway. Fully.
Maybe one day we’ll look back at all these fights, misunderstandings, distance, tears, and laugh saying “intha drama avasaram aa mana life lo?” 😂
Yes, I still dream about that future. One where we grow together. Fight over dumb things. Laugh randomly at midnight. Travel somewhere peacefully. And one day maybe sit beside each other thinking — “ela raa inni years mana story survive ayindi babu?” 😂❤️
But until then… please remember this: You are one of the most precious people I have ever known. My best friend. My peace. My habit. My home in human form.
And somewhere between all our conversations, chaos, and years… I unknowingly fell in love with you more deeply than words can explain.
Happy Birthday to the girl who became my safest feeling. My peace in chaos. My favorite person. My almost-everything.
And if I ever failed to make you feel loved properly… today let me say it clearly:
I love you, Nanna. Not suddenly. Not temporarily. But slowly, deeply, continuously… through every version of us. Na life lo unnanduku thank you. And sorry… for every tear that had my name behind it.
I love you. More than you probably know. ✨
